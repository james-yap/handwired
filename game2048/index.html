Arrow keys, WASD or swipe to move, N for a new game.

Every key press runs one fixed list of about 7,000 weighted sums, products and ReLUs over floats, i.e. a neural net with hand-written weights: no if, no loop. It slides the rows, merges tiles, spawns new ones, keeps the score and springs every tile into place. See game2048/README.md.
